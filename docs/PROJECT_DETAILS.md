# ASTER — Project Details

## Project overview

ASTER — Abandoned But Not Forgotten is an interactive webcomic in three chapters, created for the NASA
Space Apps Challenge. Illustrated scenes, character dialogue, sound and mini-games connect a fictional
future expedition with the legacy of real space missions.

## Created by our team

We created ASTER’s story from scratch, including its characters, personalities, relationships and
narrative. Our team established the visual direction, drew the original Mara and Eli character references,
and designed the mini-game concepts.

## Original concepts, assisted illustrations

Pip and the spacecraft were conceived by our team. Their illustrations were produced with AI assistance
rather than drawn entirely by hand by our team.

## AI-assisted artwork

We used generative image tools to create backgrounds and additional character poses, guided by our
references and visual direction. This includes the painted star field and Pip’s pose carrying Sojourner.
AI tools also helped prepare selected visual assets, such as our logo, for use on the website.

The painted star field and Pip’s carrying pose were made with OpenAI image generation using existing
project artwork as references. The logo artwork was supplied by the team and prepared for the web with
image processing. We do not claim that all illustrations were drawn by hand.

## AI-assisted development

AI coding tools, including OpenAI Codex and Anthropic’s Claude through Claude Code, helped write and
refine the website’s code under the team’s direction: the story player, interactive activities, scene
transitions, responsive layouts, animations, bug fixes and temporary placeholder graphics labelled on screen.

ChatGPT also helped structure development requests and draft explanatory project text. These tools
are production assistants, not sources of historical mission data. The team’s creative authorship and
the tools’ production assistance are described separately here; we do not claim that all code was written
without AI assistance.

## Fact and fiction

Apollo 11, Lunokhod 1, Mars Pathfinder and the Mars rovers are real missions. Their historical information
and archive images are credited in the source sections. Aster, its crew, the lunar refuelling stop, the
human Mars expedition and the recovery of Sojourner are fiction, not NASA operations or plans. NASA does
not endorse this project.

Generated illustrations are artistic interpretations, not NASA photographs or mission records. The
APXS spectrum in the rover activity is an illustrative animation, not measured mission data.

## Sound and typography

Character voice blips, story cues and ambient music are synthesized in the browser by the website’s code.
A legacy click sample remains in the project; its origin is not documented.

Barlow Condensed is used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the license is included in
`public/assets/fonts/OFL.txt` and linked in the website’s Project Details panel.

## Data and source attribution

**NASA Data** lists the NASA mission information and imagery used in the story, including NASA mission
imagery hosted by a mission partner. Historical references and photograph provenance are recorded in
`docs/NASA_SOURCES.md` and the asset manifest.

**Space Agency & Other** lists used materials from other providers: the Wikimedia Commons photograph of
the Lunokhod 1 museum model, used in Chapter 01’s lunar archive, and Natural Earth coastline geometry,
used in the fallback Earth illustration. These are not NASA-produced materials.

The website’s Credits & Sources panel contains the actual source links and their attribution. Development
tools are described in Project Details and are not linked as data sources. Font licensing is also kept in
Project Details rather than presented as scientific data.

## Terminology

We use AI here for clarity. NASA’s current communications also use the term Super Intelligence (SI).
This terminology does not imply NASA endorsement or certification of ASTER or its tools.
