CHAPTER 1 — WHERE IT ALL BEGAN
Estimated duration: 2 minutes
Scene 1 — The Journey to the Moon
The story begins in the late 21st century.
A spacecraft called Aster is approaching the Moon.
Its crew is traveling to Mars as part of humanity's first mission to establish a permanent settlement on the Red Planet.
Inside the spacecraft, Captain Mara is reviewing the flight route, engineer Eli is looking at Earth through a window, and Pip is checking the ship's systems.
Eli: "We're going all the way to Mars, and I still don't know if they have decent coffee there."
Mara: "Six years of preparation, and you're worried about coffee?"
Eli: "Everyone has their priorities."
Pip: "According to medical research, excessive caffeine consumption can—"
Eli: "Pip, don't start."
Pip: "Understood. Disabling concern for your health."
Mara smiles.
Suddenly, the spacecraft's computer announces that they are approaching the Moon.
The camera moves outside, revealing a massive futuristic lunar base on the surface.
The spacecraft begins its descent.
Scene 2 — The Legacy of Apollo
While their spacecraft is being refueled, the crew decides to visit the historic Apollo 11 landing area.
Eli wants to show Mara and Pip where humanity's journey beyond Earth truly began.
As they travel across the lunar surface, they notice the old descent stage of the Apollo 11 lunar module in the distance.
Mara: "Is that really Apollo 11?"
Eli: "Yep. More than a hundred years ago, humans landed here for the very first time."
Pip begins scanning the structure.
Pip: "Object identified. Age: over one hundred years. Technical condition: outdated. Replacement recommended."
Eli: "Pip, that's a historical artifact!"
Pip: "Understood. Extremely old equipment that humans refuse to throw away."
Mara: "Exactly."
Pip: "Humans are very strange."
Interactive Moment — Apollo 11 Flashback
The visitor clicks on the Apollo 11 landing site.
The modern lunar environment slowly transitions into historical footage and photographs from NASA's Apollo 11 mission in 1969.
We briefly see the first human steps on the Moon through an animated historical reconstruction.
Then the scene returns to the future.
For a few seconds, the characters remain silent.
Mara: "They probably never imagined how far we'd eventually go."
Eli: "Neither can we."
Pip looks at the old lunar module.
Pip: "So this object is still important, even though it no longer works?"
Eli: "Exactly."
Pip pauses.
Pip: "Interesting logic. Saving to memory."
The crew returns to their spacecraft.
The camera follows Aster as it leaves the Moon and begins its journey toward Mars.
END OF CHAPTER 1
